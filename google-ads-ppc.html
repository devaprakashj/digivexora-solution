Common questions

What is the difference between Google Ads and SEO?

Ads start working once approved and stop when the budget stops. SEO takes longer but builds lasting visibility. Many businesses use both.

Who pays Google?

You pay Google directly for ad spend. Our fee is separate.

Will I pay for clicks from uninterested people?

Some, which is why we add negative keywords and review search terms regularly.

How soon will I see results?

Traffic can start within days of launch, but the first weeks are for learning and tuning.

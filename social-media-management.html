Common questions

Which platforms do you manage?

We recommend platforms based on where your customers spend time. That is commonly Instagram and Facebook, with LinkedIn for business-to-business brands.

Do I get to approve posts?

Yes. You see the calendar and designs before anything goes live, unless you ask us to publish directly.

How many posts will you publish?

It depends on your package and goals. We agree a realistic schedule instead of posting for the sake of it.

Do you also run paid ads?

Paid campaigns are a separate service, and the two work well together. See our Meta Ads service.

Common questions

What file formats do I receive?

Print-ready PDFs, and PNG or JPG for web and social. Editable source files can be shared when we agree it in advance.

How many revisions are included?

We agree the number of revision rounds up front so timelines stay clear.

Can you work from my existing logo?

Yes. Send your logo and brand colours. If you do not have them yet, ask us about a brand kit.

Do you handle the printing?

We design the artwork and can advise on print specifications, then send files to your printer.

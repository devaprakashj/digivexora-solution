Common questions

How much ad budget do I need?

It depends on your market and goal. We suggest a starting budget and scale only when results justify it. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and is separate from our service fee.

Can you promise a number of leads?

No. Results depend on your offer, market and budget. We promise careful setup, structured testing and honest reporting.

Do I need a website?

A landing page or lead form helps. We can use Meta's built-in lead forms or build a dedicated landing page.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your account so you keep access to everything.

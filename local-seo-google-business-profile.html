Common questions

Do I need a physical shop?

Google Business Profile suits businesses that meet customers in person or travel to them. Rules differ for service-area businesses, so we check your situation against Google's guidelines first.

Can you remove a bad review?

Only reviews that break Google's policies can be reported, and Google makes the decision. We help you reply professionally and build up genuine reviews.

Will you buy or post fake reviews?

No. Fake or incentivised reviews break Google's rules and can get a profile suspended.

How long until I see a difference?

Profile improvements can show quickly. Stronger local visibility builds over weeks and months.

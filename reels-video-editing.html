Common questions

Do you shoot the videos?

Our service is editing and post-production. If you need filming too, tell us what you have in mind and we will say what we can arrange.

How long does editing take?

It depends on the length and number of videos. We confirm a turnaround before starting.

Can you add captions in my language?

Tell us the language and share the script if you have one, and we will confirm what we can do.

What about music?

We use music that is licensed for social platforms where possible. Trending audio usually has to be added inside the app when you publish.

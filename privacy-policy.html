Privacy policy

Last updated 01 November 2026.

This page explains what information Digivexora Solution collects through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, business name (optional), email address (optional), the service you are interested in and your message. We also record which page you submitted the form from.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry and to discuss our services with you. We do not sell your information.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in a database on our web hosting. Access is limited to people who need it to respond to you. We keep enquiries only for as long as needed to follow up and manage our business relationship.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads. This website does not set advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing digivexorasolution@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Common questions

How long does a website take?

A focused business website often takes a few weeks. It depends on the number of pages and how quickly content and feedback arrive.

Do you write the content?

We can help structure and edit your content, and write copy as an add-on if you need it.

Will I own the website?

Yes. You own your domain and your content.

Can you improve my existing site instead of rebuilding?

Yes. We review what works first, then improve or rebuild only what is needed.
